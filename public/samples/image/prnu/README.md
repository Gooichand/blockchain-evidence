C01, C02 and C15 are three different smartphones. flat-* are flat-field
images (fingerprint reference); nat-* are natural photos (queries). C01 is a
genuinely weak-PRNU modern device: its own paper documents limited PRNU
usability on modern computational-photography pipelines, and our measurements
agree - it is kept as a disclosed weak case, not a hidden miss.
