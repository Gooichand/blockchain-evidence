What is This System?

This is a Blockchain Evidence Management System designed to securely store, verify, and track digital evidence using blockchain immutability and cryptographic verification.

Why Blockchain?

  • Immutable Records: Once evidence is recorded, it cannot be altered.
  • Transparent Audit Trail: Every action is permanently logged.
  • Hash Verification: Any tampering is instantly detectable.

Key Roles in the System

  • Investigator: Uploads and manages evidence.
  • Forensic Analyst: Reviews and verifies evidence.
  • Court Official: Accesses verified evidence for legal proceedings.
  • Administrator: Manages users and system settings.
