Investigator

Collect and preserve digital evidence from crime scenes and secure it for blockchain verification

Responsibilities

  • Secure crime scene digital evidence collection
  • Create and manage investigation cases
  • Upload evidence with metadata and timestamps
  • Initiate chain of custody records

Allowed Actions

  • Create new case files
  • Upload evidence files
  • Add case notes and annotations
  • View assigned case evidence
  • Request forensic analysis

Restricted Actions

  • Modify blockchain records
  • Delete uploaded evidence
  • Access other investigator cases
  • Approve court submissions
  • Modify system configurations

Security & Integrity Layer

Every action is protected by multiple security layers

SHA-256

Cryptographic hashing creates a unique digital fingerprint for every piece of evidence

IPFS

Decentralized storage ensures evidence is distributed and cannot be lost or altered

Polygon Blockchain

Immutable ledger records every transaction with timestamp and validator confirmation

Audit Trail

Every action is logged with user identity, timestamp, and cryptographic signature

Chain of Custody

Complete traceability of evidence movement with handover verification at each step

Role Based Access

Granular permissions ensure users can only access what their role authorizes

Public Transparency

The Public Viewer role allows journalists, oversight committees, and the public to verify evidence integrity without accessing sensitive case details.
