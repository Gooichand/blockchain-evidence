Noise Analysis moved to strict on November14: PRNU same-device attribution compares a Gaussian-residual fingerprint built from user-supplied reference photos against the query image's residual (center 512px crop, peak correlation and PCE over +/-4px alignment shifts). Validated on the PrnuModernDevices real-camera dataset (CSP Lab, University of Florence, CC BY-SA 4.0): devices C02 and C15 separate same-device naturals from cross-device fingerprints by PCE and correlation margins; the weak-PRNU device C01 is disclosed as a documented weak case, not credited. Scaled, cropped, rotated or re-encoded images can erase the signal and are stated misses; output is a measurement-only candidate signal, never a match verdict or person identification (PRNU-ATTRIBUTION-2026-11-14.md).
